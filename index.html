Key achievements

What makes this work stand out

Cross-cutting engineering decisions that shipped to production and run in the real world.

🤖
AI-Powered Ride Audit
Integrated Claude AI (Anthropic) to parse inbound ride emails from Zoho Mail, extract structured trip data, and reconcile against booked rides — cutting manual audit effort significantly.
⚡
Real-Time Architecture
WebSocket-driven live ride monitoring across both dashboards — real-time driver location streaming and status updates pushed to all connected clients instantly.
🔒
Idempotency Framework
Built a SQL-backed annotation-driven idempotency layer (@Idempotent) preventing duplicate ride-action processing across distributed and retried requests.
📱
Phone Number Masking
Integrated Exotel Exophone to assign virtual phone numbers per ride — drivers and passengers communicate without revealing real numbers to each other.
💰
Vehicle Downgrade Waiver
Designed and shipped charge waiver logic across both services — when a lower-tier vehicle is assigned, cancellation and No Show charges are automatically waived in real time.
🗺️
Geo-Intelligence Layer
Polygon-based region assignment, point-in-polygon validation, geo-distance scoring in the allocation pipeline, and H3-cell MoveInSync tracking integration.
